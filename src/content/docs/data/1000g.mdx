## Data and windows

- **Call set.** The phased high-coverage (30×) call set of the 1000 Genomes Project, GRCh38.
- **Populations.** 25 of the 26 populations, each with 64 unrelated individuals; ASW has 61 and was
  left out. NeCast reads disjoint groups of 32: two groups in 18 populations and one in seven.
- **Windows.** 30 Mb of accessible sequence under the strict mask, within chromosome arms, with
  gaps of at most 1 Mb: 51 windows on chromosomes 1–18.
- **Sites.** Biallelic SNVs; positions with more than one record dropped; the reference allele
  taken as ancestral.
- **Maps.** The deCODE 2019 recombination map and the Roulette mutation map, cut to each window.

## Four map inputs

| folder | recombination | mutation |
|---|---|---|
| `no_map` | flat at the genome-wide mean | flat |
| `mutation_map` | flat at the genome-wide mean | Roulette, rescaled to the window's mean rate |
| `recombination_map` | deCODE 2019 | flat |
| `both_maps` | deCODE 2019 | Roulette, rescaled as above |

## Low coverage and 30×

For YRI, CEU, CHB, GIH and PEL, the first group of 32 individuals was also extracted from the
phase 3 call set (low coverage, mean depth 7.4×) on the same 51 windows, and NeCast and phlash were
run on both call sets (`low_coverage/`).

The [dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Larrivhan/NeCast-1000G) describes every folder.
