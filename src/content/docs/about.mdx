## Citation

The NeCast paper is in preparation; its reference will be added here. Until then, please cite the
repositories you use:

```text
Han Y, Wang K. NeCast simulations, benchmark and 1000 Genomes analyses.
Hugging Face: Larrivhan/NeCast-simulations, Larrivhan/NeCast-benchmark, Larrivhan/NeCast-1000G.
Trained network: Larrivhan/NeCast.
```

The windows build on msprime (Baumdicker et al. 2022), the deCODE 2019 recombination map
(Halldorsson et al. 2019), the Roulette mutation rates (Seplyarskiy et al. 2023) and stdpopsim
(Adrion et al. 2020), and NeCast reads posteriors of PSMC+ (Cousins et al. 2024).
